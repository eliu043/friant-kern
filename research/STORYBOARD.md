# Seven-chapter storyboard — November 2026 revision

Reader question: What happens to a gravity-fed canal when the ground beneath it moves unevenly?

| Chapter | Story purpose | Distinct evidence / visual state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · The route | Establish gravity and the dependence on slope. | Full reference alignment; regional shaded terrain replaces the former schematic. |
| 02 · The terrain | Locate the canal in actual regional relief. | USGS 3D elevation and a map-linked west–east section. Approximately 200 m ground spacing; not a canal-bed profile. |
| 03 · The ground | Reveal spatial variation in movement. | Hide population by default; add the dated cumulative displacement raster. |
| 04 · The close-up | Quantify three illustrative observations. | Add sample dots; corresponding HTML values and proportional bars. |
| 05 · The bottleneck | Explain the operational consequence. | Desktop full-width metric scene, historical >60% loss in the critical 10-mile Phase 1 segment. Mobile retains sticky map above the metric. |
| 06 · The repair | Distinguish completed work from the larger project. | Resume the reference map with displacement; source-linked 2020–2024 timeline. No invented repair extent. |
| 07 · The longer story | Separate infrastructure repair from lasting aquifer compaction. | Leave the map for the closing argument about potentially permanent storage loss. The 5 ft allowance belongs to repair, not storage. |

The opening uses a quiet canal photograph and system-scale figures. Construction imagery is reserved for the full-width repair scene. Typography and whitespace follow a shared editorial system.

Every chapter sets a complete reversible map state. Layers controls allow readers to compare populations, displacement, and street context. Reset restores the active chapter's defaults. Camera framing repeats when evidence changes at the same scale; scrolling is not represented merely as zoom.
